Privacy

Privacy and responsible contact

This notice explains the approach to information submitted through professional enquiries and newsletter forms. It is not legal advice.

Information you provide

If you submit a professional enquiry, the site may collect your name, email address, organisation, enquiry category and message. If you subscribe to the newsletter, it may collect your email address and any consent or confirmation records required by the chosen email platform.

How information is used

Information should be used to respond to enquiries, administer requested subscriptions, maintain security and operate the website. Do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis and consent where required.

Service providers

Hosting, form handling, email marketing and analytics services may process information under their own privacy terms. Provider-specific practices depend on the services used by the website.

Retention and rights

Retain information only for an appropriate period and establish a process for access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent where applicable. Newsletter messages should include a functioning unsubscribe mechanism through the chosen platform.

For privacy questions, contact hello@femiorimoloye.com.
