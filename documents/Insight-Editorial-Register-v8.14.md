## Editorial source policy

- Inline citations should link to original research, official guidance or the publishing organisation’s source page.
- Do not use an organisation’s name in a reference list unless the linked source actually supports the adjacent point.
- Do not imply causation from correlation or treat illustrative scenarios as real case studies.
- Jurisdiction-specific legal topics (including pay equity) require local legal review before publication.
- This build adds substantive analytical sections and source guidance; it does not replace final human fact-checking or legal review.
