# Changelog — v8: Industry Authority Integration

## Purpose
Integrate the Industry Authority concept throughout the existing Femi Orimoloye website while preserving its established visual identity, page architecture and source-grounded positioning.

## Changes
- Rebuilt `/industries/` as a substantive Industry Authority page with a clear editorial introduction, documented experience, six priority industry contexts, additional potential sectors, a context-led approach and relevant conversion paths.
- Explicitly distinguishes documented experience from transferable relevance and potential sector applicability. The site does not imply that Femi has worked in every listed sector.
- Added a homepage industry preview and links to the full Industries page.
- Added Industries to primary navigation across HTML pages.
- Added contextual links from Expertise, Services, Insights and Experience.
- Added an optional industry/operating-environment selector to the Contact form.
- Expanded the on-site search index with industry pages and sector terms.
- Added responsive card layouts, evidence labels, sector tags and reduced-motion support.
- Updated Industry page metadata for search and social previews.

## Intentionally not claimed
- No new industry employment, client engagements, testimonials or case studies were invented.
- Sector-specific pages are not generated for secondary sectors; they remain grouped as potential operating environments until there is enough verified evidence and useful content.
- This package is not deployed to the live domain. Hosting configuration, form delivery and live-browser visual acceptance remain separate verification tasks.
